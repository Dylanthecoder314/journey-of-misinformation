# Journey of Misinformation

Play it here: **https://dylanthecoder314.github.io/journey-of-misinformation/**

A browser game for an NSW Freedom to Learn project (2026, "Myth, Mystery and Misunderstanding"). You play a reporter in a top-down pixel-art world: walk the scene, talk to witnesses, film what happens, then decide what the public sees. The public reacts to your version of events, not to what really happened.

Chapters: the JFK assassination (1963–2026), 9/11 conspiracy theories (2001–2026), Malaysia Airlines flight MH370 (2014–2026), and an imaginary lake town where Fama, the goddess of rumour, grows with every scary story. Story mode follows one newspaper from 1963 to 2026.

Works in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks, on keyboard, mouse or touch. Everything runs in the page; play-test answers stay on the device.

Inspired by Nicky Case's [We Become What We Behold](https://ncase.me/wbwwb/). All news outlets in the game are fictional. The real-event chapters never show violence, the attacks or a crash, and each one ends with what the evidence shows, plus sources.
